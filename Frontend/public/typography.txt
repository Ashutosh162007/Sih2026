SAHAYOG — NAVIGATION TYPOGRAPHY (SIDEBAR / LEFT MENU GUIDE)
Every navigation button (word(s)) on the left-hand sidebar,
role by role, and what each one does.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
COMMON NOTE
-------------------------------------------------------------------
The left navigation is the vertical menu shown inside the app shell.
Each role gets its own list of buttons. Some buttons are shared
across roles (State Map, Showcase, Help, Workflow), some belong to
a single role.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CITIZENS (Citizen & Community Reporter)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Both citizen and community_reporter roles use the SAME left menu.

  Dashboard
    Route: /citizen/dashboard
    What it does: Personal home screen. Summary of your reported
    issues, their current status (submitted / in solution / resolved),
    and recent platform activity relevant to you.

  My Reports
    Route: /my-issues
    What it does: The full list of every civic issue you reported,
    with status badges and details. Open any one of them to follow it.

  Report Issue
    Route: /report
    What it does: The issue intake wizard. You describe the problem
    briefly, attach a photo, pick a location, and the AI formulates the
    formal problem statement + severity before submission.

  State Map
    Route: /map
    What it does: Interactive GIS map of the whole state. Browse
    reported issues by district/block, view severity and category
    spread, and see where problems are clustered.

  Showcase
    Route: /showcase
    What it does: A public gallery of funded innovations - issues that
    universities solved with industry funding. Shows the project,
    the funding partner, and an optional CSR impact certificate.

  Help
    Route: /help
    What it does: Frequently asked questions, how-to guides, guidance
    on using the platform, and a form to submit a support inquiry.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
2. UNIVERSITIES / HEIs
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Universities (role: university) get their own left menu.

  Dashboard
    Route: /university/dashboard
    What it does: Home screen for the university account. KPIs for
    analysed problems, team proposals, and funded projects.

  Local Issues
    Route: /university/queue
    What it does: The queue of issues AI-routed to your university
    based on proximity (haversine) and severity. This is where teams
    pick problems to work on.

  Projects
    Route: /university/projects
    What it does: List of your university's projects. Each project has
    a proposal wizard link where you can draft and submit a solution
    proposal for industry funding.

  Workflow
    Route: /workflow
    What it does: Per-project Visual Workflow Notes. Your team draws a
    whiteboard (freehand, shapes, arrows, text) documenting the
    project, and manages suggestions submitted by the industry partner
    (Pending -> Reviewed -> Accepted/Rejected).

  State Map
    Route: /map
    What it does: Interactive GIS map of the state - browse routed
    issues and their distribution, useful for choosing what to take up.

  Showcase
    Route: /showcase
    What it does: Public gallery of funded innovations - showcases your
    completed/solved projects to the state network and funders.

  Help
    Route: /help
    What it does: Platform FAQs, guides and a support inquiry form.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
3. BUSINESSES / INDUSTRY PARTNERS
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Industry partners (role: industry) get their own left menu.

  CSR Dashboard
    Route: /industry/dashboard
    What it does: Home screen for the industry account. CSR/ESG
    analytics for your involved projects - funded amount, milestones,
    and social impact tracked.

  Proposals
    Route: /industry/queue
    What it does: The proposals submitted by universities awaiting your
    decision. Review each problem + proposed solution and either fund
    it (with a deadline & milestones) or reject it.

  Funded
    Route: /industry/projects
    What it does: List of the projects your organisation is currently
    funding, with progress and milestone tracking.

  Workflow
    Route: /workflow
    What it does: Per-project Visual Workflow Notes. You can VIEW the
    university's whiteboard canvas and submit SUGGESTIONS (notes to
    the university team) but cannot draw on it yourself.

  State Map
    Route: /map
    What it does: Interactive GIS map of the state - browse the spread
    of civic issues to guide CSR priorities.

  Showcase
    Route: /showcase
    What it does: Public gallery of funded innovations - includes your
    funded projects and CSR impact certificates.

  Help
    Route: /help
    What it does: Platform FAQs, guides and a support inquiry form.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
4. ADMIN / GOVT OVERSIGHT
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Admins (role: admin) represent government oversight. Their menu is
focused on monitoring and governance.

  Analytics
    Route: /admin/dashboard
    What it does: Government dashboard with KPIs, district filter,
    reported vs resolved bar chart and category pie chart. Includes
    CSV export and a printable summary report.

  Approvals
    Route: /admin/verify-accounts
    What it does: Approve or reject pending account sign-ups
    (universities and industry partners) before they can operate
    on the platform.

  Workflow
    Route: /workflow
    What it does: Per-project Visual Workflow Notes - VIEW ONLY.
    Admin can inspect any project's whiteboard canvas and the
    suggestions exchange, but cannot draw or manage suggestions.

  State Map
    Route: /map
    What it does: Interactive GIS map of the whole state - governance
    view of issue density, categories and severity across districts.

  Help
    Route: /help
    What it does: Platform FAQs, guides and a support inquiry form.

  NOTE: Showcase is intentionally NOT shown to admins.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
5. COMMON / OUTSIDE THE SIDEBAR
-------------------------------------------------------------------
These screens exist outside the left role menus but are part of the
shell experience:

  Profile (top-right avatar)  Route: /profile
    Your account details, organisation info, and editable profile.
  Sign out (top-right)        Returns to the public home page.
  Workflow                    Appears for university, industry & admin.
